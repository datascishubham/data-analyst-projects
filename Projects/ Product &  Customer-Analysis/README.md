# Product & Customer Analytics | SQL, Python, Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed **1M+ e-commerce transaction records** to evaluate product performance, customer behavior, order trends, and revenue drivers.  
This project covers the complete data pipeline — from data extraction and cleaning to analysis and interactive dashboard creation.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **SQL** — Data extraction, aggregation & complex analysis
- **Python** (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn) — Data cleaning, EDA & visualization
- **Power BI** — Interactive dashboard
- **DAX** — Calculated measures and KPIs

💡 Key Insights

REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER is the highest revenue-generating product.
World War 2 Gliders leads in quantity sold.
United Kingdom contributes the majority of total revenue, showing high market concentration.
A small group of customers generate very high sales, while some show significant return activity.
Strong growth observed across Revenue, Orders, and Quantity.


📈 Recommendations

Focus marketing and inventory on top revenue products like REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER.
Investigate high-return customers and products to reduce reverse logistics cost.
Explore opportunities to increase sales in other countries (Netherlands, Germany, France) to reduce dependency on UK.
Analyze low-revenue but high-quantity products for upselling or bundling opportunities.
Monitor high-value customers and create loyalty programs to increase repeat purchases.
